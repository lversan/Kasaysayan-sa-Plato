Pancit Batil Patung

Prep 20 min. Cook 45-60 min. Serves 4-6

Rich stock (batil broth base)

  • 1 lb (½ kg) beef or carabao bones with trimmings
  • 6 to 8 cups water
  • 1 beef bouillon cube
  • 1 medium white onion, cubed
  • 1 small carrot, cubed
  • 1 stalk celery, chopped
  • 1 fresh egg, beaten (reserved for completing the broth later)

Meat and vegetable toppings

  • 200 g minced carabeef (carabao beef) or regular ground beef
  • 100 g pork liver, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup crushed pork chicharon or lechon carajay bits
  • 1 cup mung bean sprouts (togue)
  • 1 cup cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 medium carrot, julienned
  • 1 large red onion, chopped (divided in half)
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced (divided in half)
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Soy sauce, fish sauce, and black pepper to taste

Noodles and patung

  • 500 g fresh miki noodles (preferably Tuguegarao-style flat noodles)
  • 4 to 6 fresh eggs (1 per serving bowl, for poaching or frying)

Dipping sauce (mandatory side)

  • 1 cup red onions, chopped
  • Philippine vinegar, soy sauce, calamansi, and crushed red chilies

Pancit Cabagan

Prep 15 min. Cook 25-35 min. Serves 4-5

Ingredients

  • 500 g fresh miki noodles (thick egg noodles)
  • 250 g pork, sliced into small pieces
  • 150 g chicken, sliced (optional)
  • 1 cup cabbage, chopped
  • 1 small carrot, sliced into strips
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon oyster sauce (optional)
  • 4-5 cups chicken broth or water
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 4-6 pieces calamansi
  • 4-6 quail eggs, boiled
  • Crispy pork lechon carajay or chicharon for topping (optional)
  • Spring onions for garnish (optional)

Sinanta

Prep 20 min. Cook 45-60 min. Serves 4-5

Ingredients

  • 200 g flat egg noodles (miki or laddit)
  • 100-200 g rice vermicelli (bihon or sotanghon)
  • 250 g chicken, cut into pieces
  • 250 g pork, cut into small pieces
  • 500 g small freshwater clams, cleaned
  • 2 tablespoons dried shrimp (hibi)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon annatto powder (atsuete)
  • 6-8 cups water or meat broth
  • Fish sauce and pepper, to taste
  • Chives or spring onions for garnish
  • Fried garlic for topping (optional)

Note: traditional family recipes vary. Some use specific small freshwater clams called ala and flat noodles called laddit.
